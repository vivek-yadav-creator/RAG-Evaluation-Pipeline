Solstice BioTech Inc. — Annual Report FY2025

Solstice BioTech Inc. is a mid-cap biotechnology company headquartered in
Austin, Texas, specializing in mRNA-based therapeutics for autoimmune disorders.

Financial Overview
Total revenue for FY2025 was $184 million, a 22% increase over FY2024's
$151 million. Net income was $12 million, compared to a net loss of $4 million
in FY2024. Research and development spending totaled $67 million, representing
36% of total revenue. The company ended the fiscal year with $210 million in
cash and cash equivalents.

Product Pipeline
The company's lead candidate, SB-401, targeting rheumatoid arthritis, completed
Phase II trials in Q3 FY2025 with a 68% response rate among trial participants,
compared to 31% in the placebo group. SB-402, a second candidate targeting lupus,
entered Phase I trials in Q4 FY2025. The company expects to file an IND
application for a third candidate, SB-403, in early FY2026.

Workforce and Operations
Solstice BioTech employed 412 full-time staff as of the end of FY2025, up from
340 the prior year. The company operates two manufacturing facilities, one in
Austin, Texas and one in Research Triangle Park, North Carolina. A third
facility in San Diego, California is under construction and expected to be
operational by mid-FY2026.

Risk Factors
The company noted in its filings that SB-401's Phase III trial, expected to
begin in Q1 FY2026, carries execution risk given the complexity of enrolling
autoimmune patients across multiple trial sites. The report also disclosed
that the company relies on a single third-party supplier for a key raw
material used in mRNA synthesis, creating supply chain concentration risk.

Leadership
The CEO stated in the shareholder letter that the company's priority for
FY2026 is advancing SB-401 through Phase III while maintaining current cash
runway through at least FY2027 without additional financing.
